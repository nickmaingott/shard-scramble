Shard Scramble

Real-time arena for 2–8 players. Grab shards. Dash into rivals to knock theirs loose.

How to play
  1. Move with WASD / arrow keys (phone: drag anywhere on the left side).
  2. Touch blue shards for +1 point, rare gold shards for +3.
  3. Press Space (phone: DASH button) to dash. Dashing into a rival makes them drop half their shards on the floor — anyone can grab them.
  4. Dash has a 2-second cooldown (the ring around you). After being hit you flash and are safe for 1.5 seconds.
  5. Round lasts 90 seconds. Most shards at the end wins.
